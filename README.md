# Supply Chain Analytics & DSML Portfolio

This repository contains my portfolio projects focused on **Supply Chain
Analytics, Data Science, and Machine Learning** applied to manufacturing
and supply chain business problems.

My goal is to combine supply chain domain knowledge with data analytics
and machine learning to support better planning and decision-making.

## Projects

### 1. Demand Forecasting & Inventory Decision Analytics

-   Analyzed historical demand and inventory data.
-   Compared demand forecasting models using Python and Scikit-learn.
-   Identified products and locations with higher historical stockout
    rates.
-   Analyzed purchase order lead times and supplier performance.

**Tools:** Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Scikit-learn

### 2. Manufacturing Performance & Predictive Analytics

-   Analyzed production delays across plants and product families.
-   Compared delay rates by order priority, material delay, and
    capacity-load group.
-   Built and compared classification models to identify production
    orders at risk of delay.
-   Tested different prediction thresholds and reviewed the trade-off
    between recall and false alarms.

**Tools:** Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Scikit-learn, Google Colab
