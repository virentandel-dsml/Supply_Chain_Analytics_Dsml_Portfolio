# Supply Chain Analytics & DSML Portfolio

This repository contains my portfolio projects focused on **Supply Chain Analytics, Data Science, and Machine Learning** applied to manufacturing and supply chain business problems.

My goal is to combine supply chain domain knowledge with data analytics and machine learning to support better planning and decision-making.

## Projects

### 1. Demand Forecasting & Inventory Decision Analytics

- Analyzed historical demand and inventory data.
- Compared demand forecasting models using Python and Scikit-learn.
- Identified products and locations with higher historical stockout rates.
- Analyzed purchase order lead times and supplier performance.

**Tools:** Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Scikit-learn
